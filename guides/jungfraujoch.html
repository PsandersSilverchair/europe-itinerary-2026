Jungfraujoch — Top of Europe

Thu Jun 25 • All day • 3,454 m
The Big Picture This is the region's "up by ingenuity" theme at its most jaw-dropping. Where Victorian mountaineers risked their lives, engineers later drilled a railway straight through the Eiger and Mönch to a saddle below the Jungfrau. The result, opened in 1912, is still Europe's highest railway station — a train ride to the roof of the continent.

📖Before You Go — The Story

In 1893, businessman Adolf Guyer-Zeller sketched a wild idea: tunnel a railway inside the mountains up to the Jungfraujoch. Construction ran from 1896 to 1912 through dynamite, hardship, and Guyer-Zeller's own death in 1899 — and on 1 August 1912 the station opened at 3,454 m (11,332 ft). Most of the 9 km line from Kleine Scheidegg is in tunnel through the Eiger and Mönch.

At the top: the Sphinx Observatory (reached by a fast elevator), a working atmospheric research station; the Ice Palace, with tunnels carved into living glacier ice; and a snow terrace looking south over the Aletsch Glacier, the longest in the Alps (~23 km) and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

One honest tip that can save your whole day: this is the trip's biggest "don't pay to sit in a cloud" risk. Check the webcams and forecast the same morning before committing — your ticket is valid Jun 25–26, so you have a backup day if Jun 25 is socked in.

🧭On Tour

Morning
Go early
First trains mean clearest air and thinnest crowds; clouds build on summer afternoons. Sit on the right going up from Kleine Scheidegg for valley views before the tunnel.
At the top
Take it slowly
Walk gently the first 30 minutes (you're high up). Sphinx terrace → Ice Palace → snow terrace.
Anytime
June snowball fight
There's snow up here even in summer.

🕵️For Levi & Micah — Spot These!

  • You're riding a train inside a mountain that took 16 years of dynamite to dig.
  • Touch a wall of glacier ice in the Ice Palace that's slowly flowing downhill.
  • The Sphinx looks like a Bond-villain lair (the real film one is the Schilthorn across the valley).
  • Snowball fight in June on the Aletsch terrace.
  • Myth-buster: "Top of Europe" is the highest railway station — not Europe's highest point (that's Mont Blanc).
