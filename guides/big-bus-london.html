The Big Picture Remember the big theme: rivers run the show. 📖Before You Go — The Story

London began as Roman Londinium, a walled town at the first easy bridging point on the Thames. For 2,000 years the river has been the city's main street — bringing in trade, invaders, and (less happily) prisoners. The boat route between Westminster and the Tower retraces the exact journey condemned prisoners once took by water to Traitors' Gate.

Your ticket is a 48-hour Big Bus pass with three routes and 50+ stops, plus a ~40-minute Thames sightseeing cruise with live commentary, plus a ~2-hour Panoramic Evening Tour (one departure a night, 7:30 PM from the London Eye stop). A heads-up: in mid-June the sun doesn't set until ~9:20 PM, so the "night" tour is really a glorious golden-hour ride.

One honest tip: the buses are a great overview, but they crawl in traffic. Treat them as sightseeing, not as transport to timed bookings — for those, the Tube is faster.
